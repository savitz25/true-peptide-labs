Research Use Only – What It Means

A plain-language explanation of the RUO designation as applied to VialVibes catalog materials for laboratory professionals and institutional buyers.

Author: VialVibes research documentation team Reviewer: — (slot reserved) Last reviewed: 2026-07-28

RUO definition (in this catalog context)

Research Use Only (RUO) means materials are supplied for laboratory research, experimental investigation, and related scientific work—not for diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention of disease, and not for human or veterinary administration.

At VialVibes (operated by APEX Research, LLC), every catalog product is positioned as RUO. Purchase and use are governed by our legal Research Use Only policy and Terms of Sale.

Not FDA-approved drugs (catalog materials)

VialVibes research materials are not marketed as FDA-approved drugs and are not intended for clinical use. They are not medical devices for diagnostic decision-making when sold as RUO research chemicals/peptides in this catalog.

Separately, the scientific literature and pharmaceutical industry may discuss approved medicines or investigational drug products with related sequences or mechanisms. Mentions of pathway names (e.g., GLP-1 receptor literature) on research pages describe experimental context; they do not convert TPL research materials into approved therapies.

Buyer responsibility

Purchasers are expected to:

  • Be qualified researchers, laboratory professionals, or authorized institutional buyers;
  • Use materials only for lawful research purposes consistent with RUO restrictions;
  • Maintain appropriate storage, documentation, and safety practices under institutional rules;
  • Not resell or redirect materials for human consumption, injection, or clinical administration;
  • Accept that analytical documentation (CoA) describes tested lots and does not authorize non-research uses.

Institutional use

Academic, industrial, and contract laboratories often require RUO materials for method development, in vitro assays, preclinical model work under institutional animal care oversight (where applicable and separate from human use), and analytical studies. Institutional policies may impose additional controls beyond supplier RUO language.

For institutional procurement questions, email research@truepeptidelabs.com or visit Contact.
