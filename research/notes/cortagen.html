Cortagen

Research context

In organotypic culture, the tetrapeptide Cortagen (Ala-Glu-Asp-Pro) was tested on rat brain-cortex explants, with explant growth as the recorded endpoint.

What has been studied

  • Organotypic rat explants

Khavinson (2001) tested Cortagen (Ala-Glu-Asp-Pro) in organotypic cultures of rat brain cortex and recorded explant growth. The same short report also tested other synthetic peptides on explants from different tissues; those other peptides are outside this note.

These labels restate the setting of the cited paper. They are not new findings and do not describe a catalog material.

Research model areas

  • Organotypic rat brain-cortex explants

Selected published literature

  • Khavinson (2001), Bulletin of Experimental Biology and Medicine · PMID 11713572

What remains uncertain

Limitations

An explant-growth observation in rat organotypic culture does not establish a human neurological application.
