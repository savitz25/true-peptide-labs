Vesugen

Research context

Published mouse work administered the tripeptide Vesugen (Lys-Glu-Asp, KED) intraperitoneally to 5xFAD mice and recorded dendritic-spine morphology.

What has been studied

  • Mouse model
  • In vitro model

Khavinson et al. (2021) administered the tripeptide Vesugen (Lys-Glu-Asp) intraperitoneally to 5xFAD mice and recorded dendritic-spine morphology. The paper also describes an in vitro amyloid synaptotoxicity model. A 2025 correction was issued for this article.

These labels restate the setting of the cited paper. They are not new findings and do not describe a catalog material.

Research model areas

  • 5xFAD mouse dendritic-spine counts
  • In vitro amyloid synaptotoxicity model

Selected published literature

  • Khavinson et al. (2021), Pharmaceuticals · PMID 34071923
  • Khavinson et al. (2025), Pharmaceuticals correction · PMID 39861198

What remains uncertain

Limitations

A 2025 correction was issued for this paper (PMID 39861198). The mouse experiment does not establish a human neurological outcome, and this note does not describe a vascular use.
