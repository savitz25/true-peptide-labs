KPV

Research context

The Lys-Pro-Val tripeptide is examined in intestinal epithelial and immune-cell experiments, including PepT1 transport and inflammatory-signaling readouts, with accompanying mouse models.

What has been studied

  • Human cell lines
  • Mouse models

Dalmasso et al. (2008) examined Lys-Pro-Val in Caco2-BBE and HT29-Cl.19A intestinal epithelial cells and in Jurkat T cells, including PepT1 uptake and NF-kappaB and cytokine readouts. The same paper recorded histologic and cytokine mRNA measurements in DSS- and TNBS-induced mouse colitis models.

These labels restate the setting of the cited paper. They are not new findings and do not describe a catalog material.

Research model areas

  • Intestinal epithelial cell lines
  • Jurkat T cells
  • Mouse intestinal inflammation models

Selected published literature

  • Dalmasso et al. (2008), Gastroenterology · PMID 18061177

What remains uncertain

Limitations

The cited cell-line and mouse experiments support a model-system research context only; they do not establish a human gastrointestinal application.
