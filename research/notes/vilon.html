Vilon

Research context

Mouse thymocyte experiments examine the dipeptide Vilon (Lys-Glu) using blast transformation and membrane sphingomyelinase activity as readouts.

What has been studied

  • Mouse thymocyte assays

Khavinson et al. (2002) examined the dipeptide Vilon (Lys-Glu) in mouse thymocytes, with blast transformation and membrane sphingomyelinase activity as readouts. The same assays included Epithalon and Cortagen as comparison peptides.

These labels restate the setting of the cited paper. They are not new findings and do not describe a catalog material.

Research model areas

  • Mouse thymocyte blast transformation
  • Thymocyte-membrane sphingomyelinase activity

Selected published literature

  • Khavinson et al. (2002), Bulletin of Experimental Biology and Medicine · PMID 12420072

What remains uncertain

Limitations

These mouse thymocyte assays do not establish an effect on human immune function, lifespan, or tumor biology.
