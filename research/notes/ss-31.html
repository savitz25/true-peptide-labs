SS-31

Research context

Isolated-mitochondria experiments with elamipretide (SS-31) measure ADP sensitivity in aged skeletal-muscle mitochondria and uptake through the adenine nucleotide translocator.

What has been studied

  • Isolated mitochondria
  • Animal physiological readouts

Pharaoh et al. (2023) measured ADP-stimulated responses in isolated skeletal-muscle mitochondria from young and old animals and ADP uptake through the adenine nucleotide translocator after elamipretide (SS-31). The same study recorded heart and skeletal-muscle function in the animal experiments.

These labels restate the setting of the cited paper. They are not new findings and do not describe a catalog material.

Research model areas

  • Aged skeletal-muscle mitochondria
  • Adenine nucleotide translocator ADP uptake

Selected published literature

  • Pharaoh et al. (2023), GeroScience · PMID 37462785

What remains uncertain

Limitations

Mitochondrial measurements in aged muscle, and the related animal physiological readouts in the same study, do not establish a human metabolic or cardiac application.
