Exendin-4

Research context

Exendin-4, a 39-amino-acid peptide isolated from Heloderma suspectum venom, was tested on dispersed guinea pig pancreatic acini, with cAMP as the recorded endpoint.

What has been studied

  • Venom isolation
  • Pancreatic acinar assay

Eng et al. (1992) isolated the 39-amino-acid peptide exendin-4 from Heloderma suspectum venom and measured cAMP in dispersed guinea pig pancreatic acini. The cited work is an isolation and acinar-cell study.

These labels restate the setting of the cited paper. They are not new findings and do not describe a catalog material.

Research model areas

  • Heloderma suspectum venom isolation
  • Guinea pig pancreatic acinar cAMP assays

Selected published literature

  • Eng et al. (1992), Journal of Biological Chemistry · PMID 1313797

What remains uncertain

Limitations

This isolation and acinar-cell study is distinct from the semaglutide catalog material. It does not establish a human metabolic application.
