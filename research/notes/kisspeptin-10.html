Kisspeptin-10

Research context

Whole-cell recordings from rat supraoptic-nucleus slices measured miniature excitatory postsynaptic currents during application of kisspeptin-10.

What has been studied

  • Rat brain slices
  • Electrophysiology

Yokoyama et al. (2014) applied kisspeptin-10 to rat supraoptic-nucleus slices and recorded miniature excitatory postsynaptic currents. The cited work is a slice electrophysiology study.

These labels restate the setting of the cited paper. They are not new findings and do not describe a catalog material.

Research model areas

  • Rat supraoptic nucleus slices
  • Miniature excitatory postsynaptic currents

Selected published literature

  • Yokoyama et al. (2014), Brain Research · PMID 25130664

What remains uncertain

Limitations

Slice electrophysiology in rats does not establish a human reproductive, endocrine, or neurological application.
