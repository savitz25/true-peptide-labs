Orexin-A

Research context

Orexin-A was identified in rat hypothalamus as a ligand for previously orphan receptors, and central administration in rats was followed by measurements of food consumption.

What has been studied

  • Rat localization
  • Food-intake measurements

Sakurai et al. (1998) identified orexin-A in the rat hypothalamus as a ligand for two orphan receptors and measured food consumption after central administration in rats. The cited work is a localization and feeding-measurement study.

These labels restate the setting of the cited paper. They are not new findings and do not describe a catalog material.

Research model areas

  • Rat hypothalamic localization
  • Central administration and food-intake measurements

Selected published literature

  • Sakurai et al. (1998), Cell · PMID 9491897

What remains uncertain

Limitations

Localization and feeding measurements in rats do not establish a human sleep, appetite, or metabolic application.
