Ghrelin

Research context

Ghrelin was purified from rat stomach as a 28-amino-acid peptide with an n-octanoyl group on serine 3, and growth-hormone release was measured in vivo and in pituitary preparations.

What has been studied

  • Peptide purification
  • GH-release assays

Kojima et al. (1999) purified a 28-amino-acid peptide from rat stomach with an n-octanoyl group on serine 3 and measured growth-hormone release in vivo and in vitro. The cited work is a purification and hormone-release study.

These labels restate the setting of the cited paper. They are not new findings and do not describe a catalog material.

Research model areas

  • Rat stomach peptide purification
  • In vivo and in vitro GH release

Selected published literature

  • Kojima et al. (1999), Nature · PMID 10604470

What remains uncertain

Limitations

Growth-hormone measurements in these experimental systems do not establish a human endocrine application.
