N-Acetyl Semax

Research context

A coordination study compares N-acetyl Semax with Semax in Cu(II) and Zn(II) binding and in SH-SY5Y neuroblastoma cultures exposed to copper.

What has been studied

  • Metal-coordination assays
  • SH-SY5Y cell culture

Magri et al. (2016) compared N-acetyl Semax with Semax in Cu(II) and Zn(II) coordination chemistry. The same paper recorded copper-exposure and zinc-distribution readouts in SH-SY5Y neuroblastoma cultures.

These labels restate the setting of the cited paper. They are not new findings and do not describe a catalog material.

Research model areas

  • Cu(II) and Zn(II) coordination assays
  • SH-SY5Y neuroblastoma cultures

Selected published literature

  • Magri et al. (2016), Journal of Inorganic Biochemistry · PMID 27586814

What remains uncertain

Limitations

This cell-culture and metal-binding study is separate from the Semax basal-forebrain note. It does not establish a human cognitive or neurological outcome.
