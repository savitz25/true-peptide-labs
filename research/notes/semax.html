Semax

Research context

A rat basal-forebrain study examines Semax binding and brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF) protein measurements in experimental neurobiology.

What has been studied

  • Rat model
  • Binding and protein assays

Dolotov et al. (2006) measured specific Semax binding on membranes from rat basal forebrain and BDNF protein in that region after intranasal application. The cited work is a rodent binding and protein study.

These labels restate the setting of the cited paper. They are not new findings and do not describe a catalog material.

Research model areas

  • Rat basal forebrain
  • BDNF and peptide-binding assays

Selected published literature

  • Dolotov et al. (2006), Journal of Neurochemistry · PMID 16635254

What remains uncertain

Limitations

Regional binding and protein measurements in a rodent model do not establish a human cognitive or neurological benefit.
