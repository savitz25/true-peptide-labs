Dihexa

Research context

An APP/PS1 mouse study of dihexa (N-hexanoic-Tyr-Ile-(6) aminohexanoic amide) recorded Morris water-maze performance, neuronal and synaptic-protein measurements, cytokine levels, and PI3K/AKT pathway readouts.

What has been studied

  • APP/PS1 mouse study

Sun et al. (2021) administered dihexa orally to APP/PS1 mice and recorded Morris water-maze performance, neuronal and SYP protein measurements, cytokine levels, and PI3K/AKT pathway readouts, including a PI3K-inhibitor comparison.

These labels restate the setting of the cited paper. They are not new findings and do not describe a catalog material.

Research model areas

  • APP/PS1 mouse water maze
  • Neuronal and SYP protein measurements
  • Cytokine and PI3K/AKT readouts

Selected published literature

  • Sun et al. (2021), Brain Sciences · PMID 34827486

What remains uncertain

Limitations

These mouse measurements do not establish a human cognitive outcome. A 2014 mechanism paper (PMID 25187433) was retracted in 2025 and is not used as evidence here.
