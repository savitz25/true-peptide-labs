Selank

Published rat frontal-cortex research examines Selank alongside GABA using neurotransmission-related gene-expression measurements.

Research model areas

  • Rat frontal cortex
  • GABA-related gene expression

Selected published literature

Volkova et al. (2016), Frontiers in Pharmacology · PMID 26924987

Scope of this note

The cited experiment measures molecular endpoints in rats. It does not establish effects on human cognition, mood, or clinical outcomes.

The cited cell-line and mouse experiments support a model-system research context only; they do not establish a human gastrointestinal application.

Hashimoto et al. (2009), Molecular Biology of the Cell · PMID 19386761

Scope of this note

Receptor and cell-survival assays are experimental endpoints. They do not establish prevention or treatment of neurological disease.
