Selank

Research context

Published rat frontal-cortex research examines Selank alongside GABA using neurotransmission-related gene-expression measurements.

What has been studied

  • Rat model
  • Gene-expression assay

Volkova et al. (2016) measured expression of neurotransmission-related genes in rat frontal cortex by real-time PCR at 1 hour and 3 hours after Selank or GABA administration. The cited work is a rat molecular study.

These labels restate the setting of the cited paper. They are not new findings and do not describe a catalog material.

Research model areas

  • Rat frontal cortex
  • GABA-related gene expression

Selected published literature

  • Volkova et al. (2016), Frontiers in Pharmacology · PMID 26924987

What remains uncertain

Limitations

The cited experiment measures molecular endpoints in rats. It does not establish effects on human cognition, mood, or clinical outcomes.

Research context

Khavinson et al. (2003), using the spelling Epithalon, added the peptide to telomerase-negative human fetal fibroblast cultures and recorded catalytic-subunit expression, telomerase activity, and telomere length. The cited work is an in vitro cell-culture study.

These labels restate the setting of the cited paper. They are not new findings and do not describe a catalog material.

Research model areas

MOTS-c

Research context

Mitochondrial-derived peptide literature examines MOTS-c in cellular metabolic assays and mouse studies of skeletal-muscle signaling and metabolic regulation.

What has been studied

  • Cell assays
  • Mouse models

Lee et al. (2015) describe MOTS-c as a peptide encoded by a short open reading frame in mitochondrial 12S rRNA. The paper reports cellular assays of folate-cycle and AMPK-related metabolic signaling and mouse experiments that recorded insulin-sensitivity and body-composition measures, with skeletal muscle discussed as a main tissue context.

These labels restate the setting of the cited paper. They are not new findings and do not describe a catalog material.

Research model areas

  • Cellular metabolic assays
  • Mouse skeletal-muscle and metabolic models

Selected published literature

  • Lee et al. (2015), Cell Metabolism · PMID 25738459

What remains uncertain

Limitations

Cellular and mouse metabolic findings do not establish human metabolic, exercise, or body-composition outcomes.
