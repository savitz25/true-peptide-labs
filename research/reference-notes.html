Selank

Published rat frontal-cortex research examines Selank alongside GABA using neurotransmission-related gene-expression measurements.

Research model areas

  • Rat frontal cortex
  • GABA-related gene expression

Selected published literature

Volkova et al. (2016), Frontiers in Pharmacology · PMID 26924987

Scope of this note

The cited experiment measures molecular endpoints in rats. It does not establish effects on human cognition, mood, or clinical outcomes.

FOXO4-DRI

Cell and mouse studies of a FOXO4-derived peptide record senescent-cell viability, p53 localization, and responses in doxorubicin-treated, fast-aging, and naturally aged mice.

Research model areas

  • Senescent-cell viability assays
  • Doxorubicin-treated mice
  • Fast-aging and naturally aged mice

Selected published literature

Baar et al. (2017), Cell · PMID 28340339

Scope of this note

Cell and mouse results are not a human anti-aging application. This entry is not assigned to a Body Explorer area.
