# VialVibes Brand System

**Tagline:** Good Vibes. Serious Science.  
**Site / repo:** true-peptide-labs (peptidetrue.com deploy)  
**Legal:** VialVibes is operated by APEX Research, LLC  

## Brand essence

Playful, premium bio-discovery platform for **research-use-only** peptides.  
Design is a trust signal — modern alternative to sterile or sketchy peptide sites.

| Outside | Inside |
|---------|--------|
| Fun, curious, energetic, approachable | Scientific, precise, transparent, trustworthy |
| Confident short headlines | RUO, CoA, literature-framed body copy |

## Voice

Smart · playful · confident · curious  
Never bro-science · never cold clinical · never sketchy  

## Logo

**VV monogram** — vial silhouette + frequency/vibe wave + subtle amino-chain nodes.  
Primary: gradient stroke Neon Mint `#00FFAA` → Lab Cyan `#00E5FF` with soft glow.  
Secondary: solid `currentColor` for single-color apps.

Assets:

- `assets/vv-monogram.svg` — primary gradient monogram  
- `assets/vv-monogram-solid.svg` — solid mono (print / light / apparel)  
- `assets/logo-vialvibes.svg` — wordmark lockup (stacked VIAL / VIBES)  
- `assets/logo-vialvibes-mark.svg` — app / favicon tile  

## Color (dark-first)

| Token | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| Deep midnight | `#0F0C29` | Page background |
| Near black | `#121212` | Alt surface |
| Elevated indigo | `#1E1B4B` | Cards / elevated |
| Off-white | `#F8F9FA` | Primary text |
| Electric cyan | `#00E5FF` | Accent 1 / links |
| Violet | `#A855F7` | Accent 2 |
| Neon mint | `#00FFAA` | Success / highlight |
| Soft gray | `#94A3B8` | Muted text |

Soft gradients, glassmorphism, subtle glows. Avoid gamer-neon overload.

## Typography

- **Display:** Space Grotesk  
- **Body:** Inter  
- **Data:** system monospace  

## Compliance (non-negotiable)

- Research Use Only language preserved from Phases 0–2  
- No inject / human protocol / therapeutic benefit claims  
- Catalog categories stay lab-framed (Tissue & Repair Models, Metabolic Research, etc.)  
- No invented CoAs, stats, or testimonials
