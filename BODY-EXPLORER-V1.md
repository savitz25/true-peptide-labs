# B1 — Body Explorer v1

Research-use-only index at `/body-explorer.html`.

- Stylized figure with five selectable research-model areas: skin and extracellular matrix, connective tissue and remodeling, gut and barrier biology, growth-hormone axis, and metabolic pathways.
- Text buttons select the same areas without using the figure.
- The side panel lists the mapped catalog compounds, a literature-framed reason each one appears, and links to the existing Research Overview, Specifications, and CoA request pages.
- Additional research-reference peptides can be added in the `BE_AREAS` list on the page.
- "Body Explorer" is in the main navigation, with a short teaser on the homepage and the Research Hub.
- Limitation statement: preclinical / laboratory research context only. No therapeutic or human-benefit claims.

Not included: 3D, new compounds, or new research articles.
