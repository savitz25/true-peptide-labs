Quality & Compliance

How we approach documentation, testing, and research-only compliance—stated carefully and without overstated claims.

Our approach

True Peptide Labs (operated by APEX Research, LLC) supplies materials intended for laboratory research. We prioritize clear research-use restrictions, lot-level documentation when available, and responsive support for qualified buyers who need Certificates of Analysis (CoAs) or related paperwork.

Quality practices can vary by product line and supplier arrangement. We describe our process in general terms below and avoid claiming certifications or accreditations we have not independently verified for publication on this site.

Analytical testing

Research peptides are commonly characterized using methods such as high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) and mass spectrometry (MS), among other techniques depending on the compound and the laboratory performing the analysis.

Where lot documentation is available, it may include identity and purity-related results reported by the testing laboratory for that lot. We provide CoAs when available for a given product lot. Availability, format, and the specific tests reported can differ by lot and product.

  • Request lot documentation via our CoA page or by emailing your lot number.
  • Review CoA data carefully for method, date, lot number, and limitations stated by the testing lab.
  • Analytical results apply to the tested material under the conditions described; they are not a guarantee of suitability for every experimental design.

What we do not claim on this page

To remain accurate and conservative, this site does not display unverified quality badges or accreditations (for example, ISO 17025 laboratory accreditation or cGMP manufacturing claims) unless and until we can document them appropriately. If you require specific quality system documentation for procurement, contact us and we will share what we can for the relevant product or supplier pathway.

We will not invent purity statistics, pass rates, institutional endorsements, or certification logos. Researchers who need documented evidence should request the CoA for their lot.

Handling expectations for research buyers

Qualified laboratories are responsible for:

  • Storing and handling materials according to product documentation and standard laboratory practice;
  • Confirming identity and suitability for their protocols (including independent verification when required by institutional policy);
  • Ensuring use remains limited to lawful research applications as described in our Research Use Only policy and Terms of Sale.

Compliance posture

Products are sold for research use only. They are not FDA-approved drugs and are not intended for human use, diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention of disease. Buyers certify age, qualification, and intended research use at purchase under our Terms of Sale.
