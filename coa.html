What a CoA typically contains

A Certificate of Analysis is documentation issued for a specific production or packaging lot. Content varies by testing laboratory and product, but researchers commonly look for the following fields. This list is educational—not a guarantee of what every document will include.

  • Lot / batch identifier — matches the lot printed on the vial or packaging.
  • Identity methods — techniques used to support compound identity (for example HPLC, MS, or other methods stated on the report).
  • Purity or related results (if tested) — only when the testing lab reports them for that lot; we do not invent or display site-wide purity percentages.
  • Date of analysis or report — when the testing or documentation was issued.
  • Lab notes or limitations — conditions, methods, and caveats stated by the testing laboratory.
