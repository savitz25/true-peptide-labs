About True Peptide Labs

Research-use peptides and laboratory tools for qualified researchers and institutions.

Who we are

True Peptide Labs is a research peptide supplier focused on serving laboratory professionals, academic researchers, and institutional buyers. We provide research compounds and practical tools—such as reconstitution calculators and research combination planning—to support controlled, in vitro laboratory work.

True Peptide Labs is operated by APEX Research, LLC. All orders, product offerings, and website content are provided under that legal entity.

What we offer

  • Research peptides intended strictly for laboratory research use only (RUO).
  • Certificates of Analysis (CoAs) when available for a given lot—see our CoA page.
  • Free research tools including a reconstitution calculator and research combination planner.
  • Email support for research and institutional inquiries.

Our principles

Research-only focus. We do not market products for human consumption, clinical use, veterinary use, or any form of bodily introduction.

Transparent documentation. When lot documentation is available, we aim to provide it upon request. Availability can vary by lot and product.

Qualified buyers. Purchasers are expected to be qualified researchers, institutions, or laboratory professionals who will use products solely for legitimate research purposes.
