What the Vial Vault is

The Vial Vault is Vial Vibes’ documentation request center for research-use-only materials. It is designed for laboratory professionals who need lot-matched Certificates of Analysis (CoAs) for procurement files, internal quality records, or experimental documentation—not a public scoreboard of invented purity metrics.

How lot documentation works

  1. 01Submit the lot printed on your vial or packaging, plus product name and a contact email when possible.
  2. 02We locate documentation for that specific lot when a CoA is available from our testing / supplier pathway.
  3. 03You receive a follow-up with the file or status. Availability, format, and test panel can differ by product and lot.
  4. 04You verify the match — lot on CoA must match lot on vial before using the document in lab records.

A browsable public PDF archive is planned for later. Until then, requests are handled on demand—honestly and without placeholder files.

Why lot-level documentation matters

  • Links analytical results to the exact material in your freezer—not a generic product page claim.
  • Supports institutional receiving, procurement, and method documentation workflows.
  • Keeps quality conversations grounded in real reports instead of marketing percentages.
  • Respects Research Use Only boundaries: documents research materials, not clinical products.

What you receive when you request a CoA

When documentation is available for the requested lot, you typically receive a Certificate of Analysis (PDF or equivalent) prepared for that lot. Content varies by testing laboratory and product. We do not guarantee a fixed test panel for every SKU or every batch.

Commonly included

  • Lot / batch identifier matching your vial
  • Identity support (e.g. HPLC, MS, or other methods stated)
  • Purity-related results if tested and reported for that lot
  • Analysis or report date
  • Method notes and laboratory limitations

Honest expectations

  • Available on request for most lots we supply—timing depends on archive retrieval.
  • Not every lot may have the same tests; always read the document you receive.
  • No instant self-serve PDF library on this page yet.
  • No site-wide purity percentages invented for marketing.
