How to Use (Laboratory Assay Prep)

  1. Select the target research amount (mg) required for your in-vitro assay aliquot.
  2. Select the vial content (mg) of the lyophilized research material.
  3. Select the reconstitution volume (mL) of bacteriostatic water or other suitable diluent added to the vial.
  4. Review the calculated concentration (mg/mL) and volume to aliquot / transfer for your experimental prep.

Concentration = vial content ÷ reconstitution volume. Aliquot volume = target research amount ÷ concentration. Values are intended for laboratory planning of research solutions only.
